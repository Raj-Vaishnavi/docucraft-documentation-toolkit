# Human fact-check checklist

## Overview

Complete this review before publishing or sharing product documentation.

## Before you begin

Use current, approved product specifications, UI builds, support guidance, and release records. Ask the responsible subject-matter expert when evidence is unclear.

## Procedure

- [ ] Confirm the product name, feature name, and supported version.
- [ ] Verify every permission, prerequisite, limitation, and default.
- [ ] Follow every procedure in the product and confirm the UI labels match.
- [ ] Confirm each stated result and status message can actually occur.
- [ ] Validate error causes and recovery actions with engineering or support.
- [ ] Check dates, version numbers, and release-note claims against records.
- [ ] Open each link and confirm its destination is correct and accessible.
- [ ] Review AI suggestions; reject unsupported claims and invented details.
- [ ] Record the source or owner for facts that are likely to change.
- [ ] Obtain required technical and editorial approvals.

## Troubleshooting

If a claim cannot be verified, remove it or mark it for follow-up. Do not publish it as fact based only on an AI response or this fictional sample.
